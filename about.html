About Uttara University CGPA Calculator

Smart, Secure & Privacy-First Academic Tool for UU Students

📌 Purpose & Overview

The Uttara University CGPA & GPA Calculator is an intelligent web application engineered to simplify academic record keeping for students at Uttara University (UU). It allows undergraduate and diploma students to compute semester Grade Point Averages (GPA), track cumulative overall CGPAs, and plan future academic target grades with 100% precision.

Built with modern web standards, this tool seamlessly aligns with Uttara University’s official 4.00 grading scale and supports both 12-semester Day shifts and 10-semester Evening / Diploma shifts.

⚡ Core Features & Capabilities

🔄

1-Click ERP Import

Paste your ERP grade report or student login credentials to auto-parse all courses, credit hours, and points instantly.

📊

Dual Mode Calculation

Switch between single semester GPA estimation and multi-semester cumulative CGPA forecasting.

🔒

Local & Private Storage

Your grades and academic data remain saved safely in your browser’s local storage. No tracking servers involved.

📱

Responsive UI Design

Fully optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktop displays with smooth bottom navigation controls.
