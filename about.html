About UU CGPA Calculator

Academic Productivity Tool for Students

UU CGPA Calculator is a student-built academic tool designed to help Uttara University students easily calculate, track, and forecast their semester GPAs and cumulative overall CGPAs.

Key Features:

  • Automatic ERP Result Sync: 1-click import of completed semester course results directly from your student portal.
  • Multi-Semester Calculation: Supports standard 12-semester Day programs and 10-semester Evening / Diploma programs.
  • Custom Grading Scale: Standard 4.00 grading scale alignment for accurate GPA calculations.
  • Privacy Focused: All calculations and data remain saved safely inside your local browser storage.
