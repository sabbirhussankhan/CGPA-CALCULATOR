Privacy Policy

UU CGPA Calculator Data Privacy & Security

1. Local Storage Privacy

Your privacy is strictly respected. All course names, grades, credit hours, and GPA calculations remain stored locally inside your web browser's localStorage. No personal data or academic marks are transmitted to external servers without your explicit action.

2. Student ERP Credentials

When logging in to fetch semester results, your credentials are processed securely solely for authentication and result retrieval. Credentials are not stored permanently on remote tracking servers.

3. Data Ownership

You retain full control of your data. You can clear or reset all stored calculator data at any time by clicking the "Reset" button inside the calculator dashboard.
